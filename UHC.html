ULTRA HARDCORE

Every last heart.

UHC stands for Ultra Hardcore: Minecraft survival with one life and natural health regeneration turned off. Players gather resources, prepare equipment, and fight to be the last player or team standing.

Protect your health

Food keeps you fed, but a full hunger bar does not restore lost hearts. Healing resources, including golden apples, become especially valuable.

Survive the world

Mobs, falls, lava, and other hazards can end a match just as surely as another player. Preparation matters before the first fight begins.

Play for the win

Seasons can be solo or team games. In team games, every member of the winning team earns a win, including teammates who died earlier.

READING THE ARCHIVE

Common terms.

Ironman
The last player to take damage in a match. Being Ironman does not require staying damage-free for the entire game.
Perfect Ironman
A player who takes no damage throughout the entire match. This requires an explicit recorded achievement.
First Damage
The first player to lose health in a match.
First Death
The first recorded death in a match, whether caused by a player or the environment.
First Blood
The player who earns the first PVP kill of the match.
Final Boss
The last player killed in PVP, according to the recorded death times. A later PVE death is tracked separately.
PVP / PVE
Player versus player, and player versus environment. PVE includes mobs and hazards such as lava, drowning, or falling.
Team Kill
Killing a member of your own team. Team Kills are counted separately from PVP kills in this archive.
Team Wipe
One player killing every member of an opposing team. The archive counts opposing teams of at least two players.
Overpowered
The data pack achievement for eating an Enchanted Golden Apple.
Rocket Science
The data pack achievement for using fireworks with a crossbow.
The End?
The achievement for traveling to the End during a match.
03

A TITLE THAT OUTLIVES A MATCH

The Crown.

The Crown is an ongoing title carried from one season to the next. Its history follows the holder and records how each new player claimed it.

01

Take the Crown

When another player kills the Crown holder, the title passes to the killer. Recorded Team Kills can transfer it too.

02

Keep the Crown

The holder keeps the title when no transfer is recorded, including seasons when the holder survives or does not play.

03

Follow the recorded ruling

Some awards use a winning player's kills, survival, health, or additional tiebreakers. The season's Crown history records the ruling used for that transfer.

Hover over a Crown in a Seasons table to read its history for that season. Click it to return to this explanation.

04

DIFFERENT WAYS TO PLAY

Gamemodes & variations.

GAMEMODE

Normal

The standard UHC format. Players begin with their assigned teams, or play individually in a solo season.

GAMEMODE

War

Two large teams face each other. The larger rosters make coordination and the survival of remaining teammates central to the match.

GAMEMODE

Find Your Team

Players begin without their full teams and form them during the match. Recorded meetings appear as timestamped Team Formed and Team Joined events in Seasons.

GAMEMODE

Mole

Secret moles start among other teams, while actually playing together on their own team. Their cover teammates do not know their identities at the start. The archive distinguishes a mole's actual team from the cover team.

GAMEMODE

Mystery

Team identities are concealed, so identifying allies becomes part of the challenge. Players must work out who belongs to their team.

MODIFIER

Roamers

A roamer begins without a team while all, or substantially all, other players start with teammates. A roamer can join a team after the match begins; recorded joins appear in the season's timeline.

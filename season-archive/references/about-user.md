# Ultra Hardcore.
UHC, or Ultra Hardcore, is a competitive, survival gamemode where players do not regenerate their health naturally. Confined within a World Border, players are pitted against one another, often in teams, in a fight to be the last standing.

While a full hunger bar will no longer heal them, players still have ways of healing: Golden Apples and Health Potions.

Some items are unable to be created, however: Strength Potions and Regeneration Potions.

The last team with a living teammate alive wins. Members of the winning team are considered winners, regardless of whether a member is alive or deceased.

# Common Terms.
## Ironman.
The last player who has not taken damage in a match will be rewarded the Ironman award. The feat is for bragging rights.

## The Crown.
Starting in Season 1, the Crown has passed between players. The Crown can pass only once per season.

The Crown passes between players through PVP. Whenever the holder of the Crown is killed by another player, the second player then becomes the Crown holder.

However, players do not always die in combat. Sometimes, they die by other means. When the Crown holder dies outside of combat, the Crown will be awarded to a winner who is alive at the end of the game, subject to several tie breakers. If more than one winner is alive at the end of the game, whichever player has more kills will be awarded the Crown. If there is still a tie, whichever player has more health will be awarded the Crown. If there is still a tie, whichever player dealt more damage to other players in the match will be awarded the Crown. If there is still a tie, the tie will be settled by a coin flip.

If the Crown holder survives the match, he retains the Crown. If the Crown holder does not play in consecutive matches, then the Crown transfers retroactively in the first match that he missed.

# Special Gamemodes.
## Ender Dragon.
Sometimes, there will be an alternative win condition: Slay the Ender Dragon.

In those matches, an End Portal Frame will be set up at X:0 Z:0 (Spawn) with all but three or four frames having Eyes of Ender inside them.

If a team or player wishes, it may collect the necessary Eyes of Ender, slot them into the frame, and slay the Ender Dragon to win the game, instead of being the last team standing.

The Ender Dragon does not need to die in order for the game to end; the game will still end if only one team remains standing. Moreover, if one team kills the Ender Dragon while other teams are still alive, the team that kills the Ender Dragon will win even despite other teams still being alive.

## Moles.
In some matches, all or most of the teams will have a mole, a false teammate who is actually on a secret team.

All of the teams are spread normally. Then, Moles are randomly and secretly selected 20 minutes into the match. The Moles retain the color of their original team, so they will blend in. When selected, the Moles will receive a quiet message that tells them their status as Moles and who is on the team.

The Mole team, like any other team, wins when their team is the last one standing.

## War.
A War game is when there are just two teams pitted against each other.

## Find Your Team.
A Find Your Team game starts with all players on their own and teamless.

When two players run into each other, a team is formed. The maximum size of a team is set before the match starts and has typically been 2 or 3 at a maximum.

## Roamer.
When a number of players arrive before the start of the game that is not easily divisible, a roamer may be created. This player is similar to a player in a Find Your Team game. He has no team and is spread separately. The first team that he comes across, he will join.
